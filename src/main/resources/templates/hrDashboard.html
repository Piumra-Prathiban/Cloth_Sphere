Generate Monthly Payroll

Payroll Generation Settings
Choose the month to generate payroll for
How it works:
• 1.0x = Regular pay rate (no overtime bonus)
• 1.5x = Time and a half (standard overtime)
• 2.0x = Double time (premium overtime)
Example: If hourly rate is $10, with 1.5x multiplier, overtime pays $15/hour
Hours beyond this count as overtime (default: 180 hours/month)
Calculation Method:
1. Hourly Rate = Monthly Salary ÷ Max Regular Hours
2. Regular Pay = Hourly Rate × (Actual Hours up to Max)
3. OT Pay = (Hourly Rate × OT Multiplier) × OT Hours
4. Gross Salary = Regular Pay + OT Pay
5. Net Salary = Gross - Deductions (EPF 8% + ETF 4%)
Note: This will generate payroll for ALL employees based on their attendance data for the selected month. Make sure attendance has been recorded before generating payroll.

Edit Overtime Multiplier

Adjust Overtime Rate
Common rates:
• 1.5x - Standard overtime (time and a half)
• 2.0x - Premium overtime (double time)
• 1.0x - Regular rate (no overtime bonus)
Important: Changing this will automatically recalculate ALL salary amounts for this employee, including gross salary and net salary.
Example Calculation:
If hourly rate = $10/hour and OT hours = 20:
• At 1.5x: OT Pay = $10 × 1.5 × 20 = $300
• At 2.0x: OT Pay = $10 × 2.0 × 20 = $400
