Internal Communication Guide

Communication Rules

Employees cannot message Factory Manager directly

For any communication with Factory Manager, please go through your department head.

All other communications are allowed

You can message HR Manager, Inventory Manager, Sales Manager, and other employees freely.

Response Time Expectations

Please allow 24-48 hours for responses during working days.

Best Practices

Clear Subject Lines

Use descriptive subject lines that summarize your message content.

Professional Tone

Maintain professional and respectful communication at all times.

Concise Messages

Keep messages clear and to the point for better understanding.

Use Reply Feature

Use the reply button to maintain conversation context.
